## ◈ Intelligence Workflow

```text
QUESTION
  └─> DISCOVER SOURCES
       └─> COLLECT & NORMALIZE
            └─> VALIDATE & PRESERVE PROVENANCE
                 └─> CORRELATE & ANALYZE
                      └─> ADD CONTEXT
                           └─> HUMAN REVIEW
                                └─> DOCUMENT & SHARE
```

AI and automation can accelerate research, but they do not replace source evaluation, analytical rigor, or human responsibility.

## ◈ Principles

- **Evidence before assertion** — distinguish verified facts, leads, and analytical judgments.
- **Provenance by design** — retain source context and traceability.
- **Correlation is not causation** — avoid overstating what the evidence supports.
- **Responsible research** — respect privacy, authorization, applicable law, and data boundaries.
- **Defensive by default** — prioritize resilience, security, and harm reduction.
- **Open collaboration** — welcome constructive review, useful documentation, and reproducible methods.
